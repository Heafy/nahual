Motion debug parameters

Turn on the "Motion debug" box under the camera to see live values, which show how the app tracks hand movement.

  • raw motion — How much your hand moved between the two most recent camera frames. Higher number means faster movement.
  • smoothed — Averaged version of raw motion. This is the number the app uses to decide when a moving letter begins and ends.
  • start/stop — When smoothed motion rises above the start value, the app begins recording a moving letter; when it drops below the stop value, the recording ends and is read.
  • state / buf — "state" is IDLE (waiting) or RECORDING (capturing a moving letter); "buf" is how many frames it has captured.
  • fps / ms det/rec — the frame rate actually processed, and the per-frame split between hand tracking (det) and recognition (rec). A healthy fps with a full buffer means no frames are being lost.
